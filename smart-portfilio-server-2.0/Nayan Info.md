### Stocker – Portfolio Intelligence Hub

Description: Multi-broker AI portfolio analytics platform built at Win Design Labs. It aggregates holdings, transaction history, and ledgers across multiple Indian and US brokers into a single view, with an agentic analytics layer (Stocker Copilot) that answers natural-language questions about portfolio performance.

Tags: AI, Agentic Analytics, React.js, Node.js/Express

Link: https://stocker-2-0-frontend.vercel.app/

Thumbnail: /images/stocker.webp

More detail:

* Hosting: frontend on Vercel, backend on Render.
* GitHub: https://github.com/superdev-windesign/stocker-2.0

Features:

* Auto Portfolio Sync
* 50+ Indicators
* Real-Time Market Data
* Daily P&L Alerts
* Live markets view: India and US markets, major indices such as Nifty 50, Sensex and Bank Nifty, equity sectors, and most active stocks, daily gainers and daily losers.
* Portfolio overview: invested and current value, unrealized and realized P&L, day's change, and a portfolio value chart, with Holdings, Analytics and Activity tabs.
* Portfolio Copilot: an AI agent that reasons over your portfolio and can compare a stock with your position and show key fundamentals.
* Rebalancing: sector targets, drift from target, and suggested trades.
* News for your holdings, with sentiment based on a headline heuristic rather than advice.
* Separate Ledger, Tax and Alerts sections, including estimated capital gains tax due for the financial year.

### AI Portfolio Chatbot

Description: Conversational RAG system built at Win Design Labs for Jitu Raut's personal portfolio site, Ultrafolio. Asking about a project renders clickable project cards, and asking about blog posts renders AI-summarized blog cards inline.

Tags: RAG, Gemini Embeddings, Supabase, Upstash Redis, MiniMax, Framer

Link: https://ultrafolio.framer.website/

Thumbnail: /images/ai-chatbot.webp

More detail:

* Full RAG pipeline: content authored in Notion, synced every 6 hours, chunked, embedded with Gemini embeddings, and stored as vectors in Supabase.
* Upstash Redis handles conversational context and response caching, and MiniMax LLM generates responses grounded in the retrieved context.
* Natural, first-person conversational tone with accurate, source-grounded answers.
* Hosting: frontend on Framer, backend (Node.js/Express) on Hostinger. The GitHub repository is private.

Features:

* Streaming Responses, Grounded Retrieval, Memory & Context Continuity, and Dynamic Projects.
* Two ways to talk to it: a chat view and a CLI view, with suggested questions in both.
* A sidebar with the project list, plus dedicated Projects and contact pages.
